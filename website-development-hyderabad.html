WordPress or custom website?

We build both and recommend what fits your business, not what is easiest for us.

WordPress

Best for most small and medium businesses, blogs and WooCommerce stores. Quick to build, easy for your team to edit, and a huge range of plugins.

Custom code

Best when you need special features, very high speed, user logins or integrations with your own software. Built with modern frameworks like React and Node.js.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Hyderabad?

At CodeServe, a Starter website costs ₹9,999, a Business website ₹19,999 and an e-commerce store ₹34,999. Domain and hosting are extra and are bought in your name, usually about ₹4,000 to ₹12,000 per year together.

How long does it take to build a website?

A Starter website takes about 7 days, a Business website 10 to 14 days and an online store 15 to 21 days, once we receive your logo, text and photos.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build is SEO-ready and submitted to Google Search Console, so Google can find and index it. Ranking on the first page for competitive searches takes ongoing SEO work and time, which we offer in our monthly Growth plan.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Business and e-commerce websites are built on an easy editor and come with a short training video, so you can change text, photos and products without coding.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We can redesign an old or slow website, move it to faster hosting and improve its SEO while keeping your existing domain.

Do I own the website after it is built?

Yes. After full payment, the website, design and content are yours, and the domain and hosting accounts are in your name.
