Pricing FAQs

Still have questions? Contact us or message us on WhatsApp.

Are there any extra costs besides your price?

Domain (about ₹800–1,500/year) and hosting (about ₹3,000–10,000/year) are bought in your name and paid by you, so you always own them. WhatsApp message charges are billed by Meta based on usage. We tell you every cost upfront before starting.

How do payments work?

50% advance to start the project and 50% before going live. Monthly plans are paid at the start of each month, or yearly with a 15% discount. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cards.

How long does a website take?

A Starter website takes about 7 days, a Business website 10–14 days and an e-commerce store 15–21 days, once we receive your content (logo, text and photos).

Will I be able to edit my website myself?

Yes. Business and E-commerce websites come with an easy editor and a short training video, so you can change text, photos and products yourself.

What is WhatsApp automation and do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

WhatsApp automation sends instant replies, order updates and reminders to your customers without manual work. We set up the official WhatsApp Business API for you and connect it to your website or store.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Every project includes free support for bug fixes (15 days for websites, 30 days for apps and AI chatbots). After that you can choose a monthly Care, Growth or Pro plan.

Can you customise a package for my business?

Yes. These packages are starting points. Message us on WhatsApp or the contact page with your requirements and we will send a custom quote within 24 hours.
