Frequently asked questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost in Hyderabad?

At CodeServe, a complete e-commerce store costs ₹34,999 one-time for up to 50 products with payments, shipping and WhatsApp order alerts. Larger or custom stores are quoted separately. Domain, hosting or Shopify plan and payment gateway fees are extra.

Should I choose WooCommerce or Shopify?

WooCommerce has lower monthly costs and more control but needs good hosting and updates. Shopify is faster to launch and needs less maintenance but has monthly and app fees. We recommend based on your products, budget and team.

Which payment gateways can you integrate?

We integrate Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe PG, CCAvenue and Stripe for international payments, with UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and optional cash on delivery.

How long does it take to build an online store?

A standard store takes 15 to 21 days once we receive your product photos, descriptions and prices. Payment gateway approval runs in parallel and usually takes a few days.

Can I manage products and orders myself?

Yes. You can add products, change prices, manage orders and run offers from an easy dashboard, and we provide a training video for your team.
