How our chatbots stay accurate

A generic AI model does not know your prices, policies or products. We use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): your documents are indexed, the chatbot finds the relevant passages for each question and answers only from them. If the answer is not in your data, it says so and offers a human instead of guessing. Read the details in our guide how to build an AI chatbot trained on your business data.

  • Works with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini models, chosen for cost and quality
  • Integrations with Google Sheets, CRMs, booking systems and e-commerce stores
  • Conversation logs and an easy way to update the knowledge base
  • Human handover on WhatsApp or email for complex questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI chatbot cost in India?

At CodeServe, an AI website chatbot trained on your website and documents starts from ₹15,000 one-time. WhatsApp AI assistants start from ₹24,999 one-time setup, and custom assistants are quoted based on channels and integrations. AI model usage and WhatsApp message fees are paid separately at actual cost.

Can the chatbot answer from my own documents?

Yes. We train it on your website pages, PDFs, brochures, price lists and FAQs using retrieval, so it answers from your information and declines questions your data does not cover.

Can a chatbot work on WhatsApp?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business API. It can reply with AI, show buttons and send documents, and hand the chat to your team when needed.

Will the chatbot give wrong answers?

We reduce this by grounding answers in your documents, limiting what the bot answers, and adding human handover. We also review conversations after launch and improve weak answers.

Can it connect to my CRM or Google Sheets?

Yes. Leads and conversation details can be sent to Google Sheets, email, popular CRMs or your own software through APIs or tools like n8n and Make.
