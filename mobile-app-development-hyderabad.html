Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build a mobile app in Hyderabad?

Our apps start from ₹74,999 for a focused MVP. Apps with bookings, payments, tracking or complex admin features cost more. We give a fixed quote after a free discussion of your features.

How long does app development take?

A simple MVP usually takes 6 to 10 weeks. Larger apps take longer and are delivered in milestones so you can test progress along the way.

Flutter or React Native: which is better?

Both build Android and iOS apps from one codebase and work well for most business apps. We recommend one based on your features, design and future plans.

Do you publish the app on the Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We prepare the store listing and publish the app. The developer accounts are created in your name: Google charges a one-time fee and Apple charges a yearly fee.

Who owns the app and source code?

After full payment, you own the app and its source code, and the store accounts are in your name.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every app includes 30 days of free bug-fix support, and we offer monthly maintenance plans for updates and new features.
