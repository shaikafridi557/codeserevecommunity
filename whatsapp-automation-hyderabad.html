Frequently asked questions

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for automation?

For reliable automation, order notifications and AI chatbots, yes. The free WhatsApp Business app only supports basic greeting and away messages. We set up the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) for you.

Can I keep using my current WhatsApp number?

Usually yes. A number used on the WhatsApp app can be moved to the Business API, but it then works through the API instead of the phone app. Many businesses use a new number for automation to keep things simple. We guide you before making any change.

What does WhatsApp charge for messages?

Meta charges per template message, with different rates for marketing, utility and authentication messages. Replies inside a conversation the customer started are generally free. Charges are billed by Meta directly to your account, based on usage.

Can WhatsApp automation connect to my WooCommerce or Shopify store?

Yes. Our WhatsApp Business plan sends order confirmations, shipping updates and payment reminders automatically from WooCommerce or Shopify.

How long does setup take?

The Starter plan takes about 3 to 5 days and the Business plan about 7 days, after Meta business verification and message template approval.
