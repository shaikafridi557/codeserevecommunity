How we protect your agency

White label only works when you can trust your partner completely. These are our standard working rules.

  • We sign an NDA before you share client details
  • No CodeServe branding, credits or footer links on your client work
  • No direct contact with your clients unless you ask us to join a call
  • Handover documents and emails written in your agency's name
  • We never approach or accept work from your clients directly
  • Fixed scope and timeline agreed before each project
  • Code, files and logins handed over to you after payment
  • A single point of contact for all your projects

How white label projects work

Frequently asked questions

Who owns the code and design?

After the project is paid in full, all code, design files and content belong to you (and your client as per your agreement with them). We don't keep any ownership rights or reuse your client's custom work.

Will you talk to my end client?

Only if you want us to. By default all communication goes through you. If you prefer, we can join client calls as part of your team, under your agency's name.

What are the payment terms?

For individual projects, 50% advance before we start and 50% on completion, before launch. Regular partners can request monthly invoicing.

How many revisions are included?

Each project quote states the revision rounds included, usually two or three. Changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately so there are no surprises.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Websites include 15 days of free bug-fix support and apps include 30 days. After that, we offer monthly maintenance plans that you can resell to your clients.

Can you work with designs made in Figma or Adobe XD?

Yes. We regularly build from Figma, Adobe XD and Sketch designs, or we can design for you if you only have a brief.
