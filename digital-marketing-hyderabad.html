Frequently asked questions

How much does digital marketing cost in Hyderabad?

Our Growth plan costs ₹4,999 per month and the Pro plan ₹9,999 per month. If you run ads, the ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta and is separate from our fee.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO usually takes three to six months to show clear results, depending on your competition and website. Google Business Profile improvements and ads can bring enquiries sooner.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings, because Google decides them. We follow proven, safe SEO practices and report progress every month.

What is a good starting budget for Google or Meta ads?

Many small businesses start testing with about ₹300 to ₹1,000 per day, then increase the budget for ads that bring good enquiries. We help you choose based on your goals.

Will I own my ad accounts and Google Business Profile?

Yes. All accounts are created in your name and we work as managers, so you keep full ownership and access.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled with notice before the next billing date.
