Frequently asked questions

How much does custom software development cost in Hyderabad?

At CodeServe, custom web applications start from ₹49,999. Portals, CRMs and larger systems are quoted after a free discovery call based on users, modules and integrations, usually delivered in stages.

How long does it take to build custom software?

A focused web application often takes 4 to 8 weeks. Larger portals and CRMs are built in stages over a few months, with working modules released every one to two weeks.

Will I own the source code?

Yes. After full payment you receive the complete source code, and hosting and accounts are set up in your name.

Can you integrate the software with our existing tools?

Yes. We integrate with payment gateways, WhatsApp, Google Workspace, accounting exports and other software that provides an API.

Should I choose custom software or a ready-made app?

Ready-made software is cheaper to start if it fits your process. Custom software makes sense when your workflow is unique, you are paying for many tools that do not connect, or you need features no product offers.
