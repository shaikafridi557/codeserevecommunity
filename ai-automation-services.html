Frequently asked questions

What is AI automation for a small business?

It means using AI and software tools to handle repetitive tasks automatically — answering common customer questions, entering data from documents, following up with leads or creating reports — so your staff spend less time on manual work.

Will the AI chatbot give wrong answers to my customers?

We train the chatbot only on your approved information and set rules for what it should not answer. When it is unsure, it hands the chat to your team. We test it with real questions before launch and keep improving it after.

Which tools do you use for workflow automation?

We use n8n, Make, Zapier or custom code depending on your budget, data privacy needs and the apps involved. n8n can be self-hosted when you want data to stay on your own server.

Is my business data safe?

We use reputable AI providers, limit what data the AI can access, and can keep automations on your own server. Accounts and API keys are created in your name so you stay in control.

What are the running costs after setup?

Usually AI usage fees from the AI provider and any automation tool subscription. For most small businesses these are modest, and we estimate them before you start.

Can you add AI to my existing website or software?

Yes. We can add chatbots, smart search, recommendations or document processing to existing websites, apps and internal tools through APIs.
