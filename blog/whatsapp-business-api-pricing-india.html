WhatsApp Business API Pricing in India (2026): Rates, Changes and Real Costs

Meta charges WhatsApp Business API messages per message, by category and by country. Here are the current India rates, what changed on 1 October 2026, and what a small business actually pays in a month.

Short answer: in India, Meta charges roughly ₹0.86 per marketing message and about ₹0.115 per utility or authentication message, plus GST. Since 1 October 2026, replies inside a customer chat (service messages) are also charged at the utility rate once a number passes 1,000 free service messages a month. Most small businesses spend a few hundred to a few thousand rupees a month on Meta's charges.

Check before you budget: Meta updates rates every few months. The figures below are the India rates published for 2026; confirm the latest numbers on Meta's official pricing page before a large campaign.

How WhatsApp API pricing works

Since July 2025 Meta charges per delivered message (it used to charge per 24-hour conversation). The price depends on two things:

  • The message category — marketing, utility, authentication or service.
  • The customer's country — a message to an Indian (+91) number uses the India rate, a message to a UAE number uses the UAE rate.

You pay Meta directly from your WhatsApp Business account. If you use a platform or a Business Solution Provider (BSP), they may add their own monthly fee or a margin per message on top.

All rates are before GST. Indian businesses are billed GST (18%) on Meta's charges, so a marketing message costs a little over ₹1 in practice. Meta also offers lower utility and authentication rates at very high monthly volumes.

What changed on 1 October 2026

Until September 2026, replies sent inside an open 24-hour customer service window were free. From 1 October 2026 Meta changed this:

  • Service messages are now charged at the same rate as utility messages — but every business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages per month. Unused free messages do not roll over.
  • Utility templates sent inside an open window are now charged as well.
  • You need a payment method on your WhatsApp Business account. Without one, service messages stop being delivered after the free 1,000.

For most small businesses the 1,000 free service messages still cover normal customer chats. Busy businesses — a restaurant taking orders on WhatsApp, a clinic handling hundreds of bookings — should now count service replies in their budget.

What is still free

  • The first 1,000 service messages per phone number every month
  • Messages customers send to you — incoming messages are never charged
  • Conversations started from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page "Send message" button, which open a free entry-point window (currently 72 hours)
  • Access to Meta's Cloud API itself — there is no subscription fee to Meta

Real monthly cost examples

1. Clinic sending reminders

600 appointment reminders (utility) + 900 chat replies (service, inside the free 1,000) a month: 600 × ₹0.115 = about ₹69, plus GST.

2. Online store

1,500 order confirmations and 1,500 shipping updates (utility) + 2,000 service replies (1,000 free, 1,000 charged) + one festival offer to 2,000 opted-in customers (marketing): (3,000 + 1,000) × ₹0.115 + 2,000 × ₹0.86 = ₹460 + ₹1,720 = about ₹2,180, plus GST.

3. Restaurant taking orders on WhatsApp

3,000 order chats (service, 2,000 above the free tier) + 3,000 order-status updates (utility) + 4 offers a month to 1,500 regulars (marketing): 5,000 × ₹0.115 + 6,000 × ₹0.86 = ₹575 + ₹5,160 = about ₹5,700, plus GST. The offers are the biggest cost — send fewer, better-targeted ones.

How to keep WhatsApp API costs low

  • Use the right category. An order update is utility, not marketing. Meta reviews templates and re-categorises wrong ones.
  • Send marketing only to engaged customers. A smaller list of people who buy beats a big list of people who block you.
  • Let customers start the chat. "Message us on WhatsApp" buttons and Click-to-WhatsApp ads bring free entry-point conversations.
  • Avoid per-message markups. Some platforms add 10–30% on every message. A direct Cloud API setup means you pay Meta's rate only.
  • Automate answers well. One clear reply with buttons costs less than five back-and-forth messages.

Messaging customers outside India

The rate depends on the customer's country, not yours. If a Hyderabad business messages customers in the UAE, UK or USA, those messages use that country's rate, which is usually higher than India's. Businesses in the Gulf can read our guide to the WhatsApp Business API in the UAE.

A simple way to estimate your monthly bill

  1. Count your monthly utility messages (order updates, reminders, payment alerts) × ₹0.115.
  2. Count your marketing messages (each offer × number of customers it goes to) × ₹0.86.
  3. Count customer chat replies; subtract 1,000; multiply the rest × ₹0.115.
  4. Add the three numbers and add 18% GST.

For most businesses, marketing messages make up more than half the bill — that is the number worth controlling.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the WhatsApp Business API cost per message in India?

In 2026 Meta charges about ₹0.86 per marketing message and about ₹0.115 per utility or authentication message for Indian numbers, before 18% GST. Service replies are free for the first 1,000 a month per number and then charged at the utility rate.

Is the WhatsApp Business API free in India?

There is no subscription fee to use Meta's Cloud API, and incoming messages are free. You pay per message for marketing, utility and authentication templates, and for service replies beyond 1,000 a month.

What changed in WhatsApp API pricing on 1 October 2026?

Service messages inside the 24-hour window became chargeable at the utility rate after a free tier of 1,000 per phone number per month, and utility templates sent inside an open window also became chargeable.

Do I pay GST on WhatsApp API charges?

Yes. Indian businesses are billed GST, currently 18%, on Meta's WhatsApp charges, and you can usually claim input tax credit if you are GST-registered.

Why are marketing messages so expensive?

Meta prices marketing messages higher to discourage spam. Sending offers only to customers who opted in and engage with you keeps costs and block rates down.
