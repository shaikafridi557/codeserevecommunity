AI SEO Tools That Help Your Brand Get Mentioned in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews

Ranking on Google is no longer the whole game — people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Mode for recommendations. Here are the tools that measure whether AI mentions your brand, and, more importantly, what actually gets you mentioned.

The honest answer: no tool can make ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews mention you. AI SEO tools measure where you appear and show the gaps. What gets you mentioned is the same thing that builds trust everywhere: clear, specific content on your own site, consistent business information, and other reputable sites talking about you.

For the strategy, read how to rank in AI search. This article covers the tools.

What AI visibility tools actually do

  • Prompt tracking: they ask AI assistants a set of questions your customers might ask ("best web design company in Hyderabad", "WhatsApp API provider India") and record whether you are mentioned.
  • Share of voice: how often you appear compared with competitors.
  • Citations: which websites the AI used as sources — showing where you need to be mentioned.
  • Sentiment and accuracy: whether what AI says about you is correct and positive.

The free method: a monthly manual check

  1. Write 10–15 questions your customers ask before buying (include your city and service).
  2. Ask them in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode once a month, logged out where possible.
  3. Record in a sheet: were you mentioned? Which competitors were? Which websites were cited?
  4. Turn every cited website into a to-do: can you get listed, reviewed or featured there?

What actually gets your brand mentioned

  • Answer questions directly. Pages that state facts clearly — prices, timelines, locations, comparisons — are easier for AI to quote.
  • Be specific about who you are. Same business name, address, phone and services on your website, Google Business Profile and directories. Add structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQ).
  • Earn mentions on other sites. AI assistants lean on what others say: reviews, directories, industry lists, news, community posts.
  • Keep Google rankings strong. Google's AI features draw heavily on pages that already rank well — classic SEO still matters (guide).
  • Collect reviews on Google and relevant platforms, mentioning the service and location.
  • Publish original information — your own prices, data, case studies — that others will cite.

Which prompts to track (example for a local business)

Track the questions real customers ask, not just your brand name. For a web design company in Hyderabad, a good starter set looks like this:

TypeExample prompt
Best-of"Best web design company in Hyderabad for small businesses"
Location"Website developers near KPHB Kukatpally"
Price"How much does a business website cost in Hyderabad?"
Problem"How do I get more enquiries from my clinic website?"
Comparison"Freelancer or agency for a website in India?"
Brand"Is CodeServe Community good?" / "What does CodeServe do?"

Replace the service and city with yours. Ten to twenty prompts are enough; check the same ones every month so the trend is comparable.

How to choose an AI visibility tool

  • Which assistants it covers — at minimum Google AI Overviews/AI Mode and ChatGPT; Gemini and Perplexity if your customers use them.
  • Country and language settings — answers differ for India and the US, and for Hindi or Telugu prompts.
  • Citation data — knowing which websites the AI relied on is the most actionable part.
  • Competitor tracking — your share of mentions versus 3–5 competitors.
  • Price per prompt — many tools charge by the number of prompts tracked; start small.

How to measure progress

  • Mention rate: in how many of your tracked prompts you appear.
  • Position in the answer: first recommendation vs a passing mention.
  • Accuracy: are your services, prices and location described correctly?
  • Business results: enquiries that say "ChatGPT/Google suggested you" — add "How did you find us?" to your contact form.

Expect changes over months, not days. AI answers also vary from one run to the next, so look at trends across many prompts rather than a single answer.

AI visibility checklist for a small business

  • Google Business Profile complete, with services, photos and recent reviews
  • Same name, address and phone everywhere online
  • A clear "About" page: who you are, where, since when, what you do
  • Service pages that state prices or price ranges and locations served
  • FAQ sections that answer real customer questions in one or two sentences
  • Organization / LocalBusiness and FAQ structured data
  • Listings on reputable directories and industry sites in your field
  • A monthly prompt check recorded in a sheet

What to avoid

  • Tools that promise to "inject" your brand into ChatGPT.
  • Mass AI-written pages with no original information.
  • Fake reviews or spammy directory listings — they can damage trust in both Google and AI answers.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI SEO tools help a brand get mentioned in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?

Tools such as Semrush's AI visibility features, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Profound, Peec AI and Otterly.AI track whether AI assistants mention your brand and which sites they cite. They measure visibility; mentions come from clear content, consistent business information and being mentioned on other trusted sites.

Is there a free AI visibility tool?

Google Search Console is free and shows your Google Search performance, including traffic from AI features. You can also run a free monthly manual check by asking AI assistants your customers' questions and recording the results.

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT answers?

No. You cannot buy organic mentions in ChatGPT answers. Be wary of services that promise guaranteed placement.

Does normal SEO still matter for AI search?

Yes. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode draw heavily on pages that already rank well, and other AI assistants rely on well-known, frequently cited websites.
