How to Build an Offshore Development Team in India (2026 Guide)

India is the world's largest pool of software talent. But there are very different ways to build a team here — from a dedicated team through a partner to your own offshore development centre. This guide compares the models, costs and steps.

By CodeServe Team · 28 November 2026 · 5 min read · Business Technology

In short: most companies start with a dedicated team through an Indian partner (fast, low risk), move to Employer of Record (EOR) hiring when they want people on their own terms, and set up their own offshore development centre (ODC) only when the team grows past roughly 20–30 people. Here is how each works and how to choose.

The four ways to build a team in India

ModelHow it worksBest forTime to start
Project outsourcingA company delivers a defined project for a fixed priceOne-off websites, MVPs, apps with clear scope1–2 weeks
Dedicated teamA partner employs full-time people who work only on your product and report to youOngoing product work, 1–15 people2–6 weeks
Employer of Record (EOR)You choose and manage the people; an EOR legally employs them and runs payroll, tax and benefits in IndiaHiring specific individuals without an Indian company2–4 weeks after hiring
Own ODC / subsidiaryYou register an Indian company, rent an office and hire directlyLong-term teams of 20–30+ people3–6 months

What an offshore team in India costs

Indian salaries vary a lot by city, skill and experience. As a rough 2026 guide for full-time software roles:

RoleTypical annual salary (India)
Junior developer (0–2 years)₹4–8 lakh
Mid-level developer (3–5 years)₹8–18 lakh
Senior developer / tech lead₹18–35 lakh+
QA engineer₹4–12 lakh
UI/UX designer₹5–15 lakh

Through a dedicated-team partner you pay a monthly rate per person that covers salary, office, equipment, HR and management — commonly in the range of US$2,000–6,000 per developer per month depending on seniority. An EOR adds a fixed monthly fee per employee. Your own centre has the lowest cost per head at scale but the highest setup cost (company registration, office, compliance, HR).

Smaller, specific needs can be cheaper still: a fixed-price website or app project avoids long-term commitments entirely — see outsourcing web development to India: costs.

Step-by-step: setting up a dedicated team

  1. Define roles and outcomes. Not "two developers" but "ship the customer portal and maintain the API". Write down the tech stack, working hours overlap and how you will measure progress.
  2. Choose the model using the table above. Start smaller than you think.
  3. Shortlist partners or candidates. Ask for similar work, speak to the actual people who will work for you, and do a paid test task.
  4. Agree the contract. IP ownership to you, confidentiality, notice periods, replacement guarantees, data protection.
  5. Onboard properly. Access, documentation, coding standards, a single point of contact and a weekly demo.
  6. Run a 4–8 week pilot with clear deliverables before adding more people.
  7. Scale once communication, quality and velocity are proven.

Making time zones work

  • UK and Europe: India is 3.5–5.5 hours ahead — a natural half-day overlap.
  • UAE and the Gulf: 1.5 hours difference — almost the same working day.
  • USA and Canada: 9.5–13.5 hours difference — agree 2–3 overlap hours and use written updates; work moves forward while you sleep.
  • Australia: 4.5–5.5 hours behind — good morning overlap.

Managing an offshore team day to day

  • Daily 15-minute stand-up in the overlap hours: yesterday, today, blockers.
  • Weekly demo of working software — the single best way to stay aligned.
  • One task board (Jira, Linear, Trello) that both sides use; no work outside it.
  • Written specs and decisions — short documents beat long calls across time zones.
  • Code reviews and automated tests before anything reaches production.
  • Visit or meet in person once or twice a year if you can — trust grows fast.

Cost example: a four-person product team

Consider a team of one senior developer, two mid-level developers and one QA engineer:

  • Hiring in a high-cost market (US, UK, Australia): salaries plus employer costs typically several times higher than India.
  • Dedicated team in India via a partner: roughly US$10,000–18,000 a month all-inclusive, depending on seniority and the partner.
  • Your own Indian entity at this size: lower salaries per head, but company setup, office, HR, accounting and compliance usually make it more expensive and slower than a partner until the team is much larger.

The savings are real, but they come from good management as much as low rates. A cheap team that needs constant rework is not cheap.

Legal and compliance basics

  • IP ownership: make sure contracts assign all code and designs to your company.
  • Data protection: India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies to personal data handled in India; also follow your own country's rules (for example GDPR).
  • Employment law: employees in India are entitled to statutory benefits such as provident fund; a partner or EOR handles this for you.
  • Own entity: setting up a private limited subsidiary involves company registration, tax registration, transfer-pricing rules and annual compliance — use a local CA firm.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing only on the lowest hourly rate.
  • Hiring many people at once with no pilot.
  • No technical lead on your side to review work.
  • Treating the team as "vendors" instead of part of your product team.
  • Unclear ownership of code, accounts and documentation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I build an offshore team in India?

Define the roles and outcomes, choose a model (dedicated team through a partner, Employer of Record, or your own offshore development centre), shortlist partners or candidates with paid test tasks, sign a contract that gives you the IP, onboard the team and run a 4–8 week pilot before scaling.

What is a dedicated offshore team?

A dedicated team is a group of full-time professionals employed by an Indian partner who work only on your product and take direction from you. The partner handles salaries, office, equipment and HR.

What is an offshore development centre (ODC)?

An ODC is your own team and office in India, usually run through an Indian subsidiary or a build-operate-transfer arrangement. It gives the most control and the lowest cost per person at scale, but takes months to set up.

How much does an offshore developer in India cost?

Full-time salaries range from about ₹4–8 lakh a year for juniors to ₹18–35 lakh or more for senior developers. Through a dedicated-team partner, all-inclusive monthly rates commonly fall between US$2,000 and US$6,000 per developer depending on seniority.

Do I need an Indian company to hire developers in India?

No. You can work through a dedicated-team partner or an Employer of Record. A company of your own makes sense only for larger, long-term teams.
