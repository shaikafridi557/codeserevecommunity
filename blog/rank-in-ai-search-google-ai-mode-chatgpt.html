How to Get Your Business Recommended in AI Search (Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini)

More customers now ask AI assistants instead of scrolling ten blue links. Here is how those AI answers pick businesses — and what you can do this month so yours is the one they recommend.

How do I get my business mentioned in AI search results?

AI search tools like Google AI Mode, AI Overviews, ChatGPT search and Perplexity build answers from pages they can crawl and sources they trust. To be recommended, keep your business name, address and phone identical everywhere, complete your Google Business Profile, earn genuine reviews, add structured data (Organization or LocalBusiness, FAQ, Product), write clear answer-first pages for each service and location, get listed on trusted third-party sites, and make sure search and AI crawlers are not blocked in robots.txt.

Why AI search matters for small businesses

Search is shifting from "show me links" to "just tell me". Google now shows AI Overviews above many results and offers a full conversational AI Mode. Millions of people also ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot questions like "best web design company near KPHB" or "good dentist in Kukatpally open on Sunday".

These tools typically name only a handful of businesses in each answer. If you are not one of them, the customer may never see your website at all. The good news: the same work that builds trust with Google also builds trust with AI systems — and most small businesses have not done it yet.

How AI answers choose businesses

No AI company publishes an exact formula, but the public documentation and what we see in practice point to a few consistent factors:

SignalWhy it matters to AI answers
Being indexed and crawlableGoogle says pages must be indexed and eligible for a normal search snippet to appear as a source in AI Overviews and AI Mode. Other assistants rely on their own crawlers or on search indexes such as Bing.
Consistent business factsWhen your name, address, phone, hours and services match across your site, Google Business Profile and directories, AI systems can confidently connect them as one business.
Reviews and reputationAI answers often summarise ratings and review counts. Zero reviews is frequently described as "unverified" or "early-stage".
Third-party mentionsListings on directories, review platforms, news sites and industry lists act as independent confirmation.
Clear, specific contentPages that directly answer a question ("How much does a website cost in Hyderabad?") with facts, prices and locations are easy to quote.
Structured dataSchema markup states your facts in a machine-readable way: address, hours, prices, FAQs.

What AI said about our own business

We tested this on ourselves. When we asked Google AI Mode for web design companies near Kukatpally, it listed competitors with hundreds of reviews — and when asked about CodeServe directly, it explained exactly why we were missing: zero Google reviews, a Maps listing without a website or phone number, a free subdomain, and a website that only said "Hyderabad" instead of our actual KPHB address.

That answer is a useful checklist for any business. Within a day we added our full address, hours and map to the website and its structured data, built a dedicated Kukatpally page, moved from the free subdomain to our own domain, codeservecommunity.in, and started fixing the profile and reviews. AI answers can be very direct about what they don't trust — so ask them about your own business and treat the response as an audit.

Try it now: ask Google AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity: "What do you know about [your business name] in [your area]?" and "Best [your service] near [your area]". Note what they get wrong or what they say is missing.

Step 1: Make your business a clear "entity"

AI systems think in entities — a specific business with known facts — not just keywords.

  • Use exactly the same name, address and phone (NAP) on your website footer, contact page, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and every directory. "KPHB Phase 9, Kukatpally" on one site and "Hyderabad" on another weakens the match.
  • Complete your Google Business Profile: primary and secondary categories, website link, phone, hours, services with descriptions, products, photos and a keyword-rich but natural description. See our Google Business Profile guide.
  • Have an About page that states who you are, where you are, since when, who runs the business and what you do.
  • Use your own domain. A free subdomain (like yourname.netlify.app or yourname.wixsite.com) can be read as a sign of a very new or informal business.
  • Link your profiles together with sameAs in your schema (shown below).

Step 2: Reviews and third-party proof

When an AI compares you with competitors, review count and rating are among the easiest facts for it to use. A business with 4.8★ from 300 reviews will almost always be preferred over one with none.

  • Ask every satisfied customer for a Google review, with a direct review link sent on WhatsApp right after the job is done.
  • Reply to every review — it shows the business is active.
  • Get listed on relevant platforms: Justdial and Sulekha for local Indian searches, Clutch and GoodFirms for agencies, Practo for clinics, Zomato/Swiggy for restaurants, IndiaMART for B2B suppliers.
  • Earn mentions: local news, association member lists, supplier "our clients" pages, guest articles and podcasts. Independent mentions are what turn "unverified" into "established".

Never buy fake reviews. Google removes them, can restrict your profile, and AI tools increasingly flag suspicious review patterns.

Step 3: Write answer-first content

AI tools quote passages that answer a question clearly and completely. Structure important pages like this:

  1. Put the direct answer in the first 2–3 sentences — who you are, what you offer, where, and from what price.
  2. Use the customer's question as a heading ("How much does a website cost in Hyderabad?").
  3. Include concrete facts: prices, timelines, locations served, opening hours, what is included. Vague marketing copy ("we deliver excellence") gives AI nothing to quote.
  4. Create one page per service and per key location instead of one long page about everything.
  5. Add an FAQ section with the real questions customers ask on calls and WhatsApp.
  6. Keep it current. Show updated dates and remove outdated prices.

Comparison and "how to choose" articles also perform well because people ask AI tools to compare options — for example our n8n vs Zapier vs Make comparison.

Step 4: Add structured data

Structured data (JSON-LD) describes your business in a format machines read reliably. For a local business, a minimal example looks like this:

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "ProfessionalService",
  "name": "Your Business Name",
  "url": "https://www.yourdomain.in/",
  "telephone": "+91-90000-00000",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "Plot 12, KPHB Phase 9",
    "addressLocality": "Kukatpally, Hyderabad",
    "addressRegion": "Telangana",
    "postalCode": "500085",
    "addressCountry": "IN"
  },
  "openingHours": "Mo-Sa 09:00-19:00",
  "sameAs": [
    "https://www.instagram.com/yourbusiness/",
    "https://www.linkedin.com/company/yourbusiness/"
  ]
}
</script>

Add FAQPage markup to FAQ sections, Product markup for products with prices, and Article or BlogPosting for guides. Test everything with Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema.org validator. Structured data does not guarantee a mention, but it removes ambiguity about your facts.

Step 5: Let the right crawlers in

If a crawler cannot read your pages, that tool cannot quote them. Check your robots.txt and your hosting firewall or CDN bot settings. Key user agents:

CrawlerUsed for
GooglebotGoogle Search, including AI Overviews and AI Mode
Google-ExtendedA control for whether your content is used to train and ground Gemini models; it does not affect Google Search ranking
BingbotBing and Microsoft Copilot; also used by several other AI search products
OAI-SearchBotShowing your site in ChatGPT search results
GPTBotOpenAI model training (separate from search)
PerplexityBotPerplexity search answers

A simple, AI-friendly robots.txt for a business site:

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /cart/

Sitemap: https://www.yourdomain.in/sitemap.xml

If you prefer your content not to be used for model training, you can block training-only agents such as GPTBot or Google-Extended while still allowing search crawlers like Googlebot, Bingbot and OAI-SearchBot. Also submit your sitemap in both Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools — many businesses forget Bing.

Is llms.txt worth adding?

llms.txt is a proposed standard: a plain Markdown file at /llms.txt that summarises your business and links to your most important pages for AI tools. Google has said it does not use it for Search, and support among other AI providers is limited. It takes ten minutes, does no harm and may help some tools — but treat it as a bonus, not a strategy. Reviews, consistent facts and good pages matter far more.

How to measure AI search traffic

  • Google Search Console: clicks and impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode are included in your normal Performance report (they are not shown separately), so watch overall trends for your key pages and queries.
  • GA4 referrals: in Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, filter session source for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com. Create a custom channel group called "AI Assistants" to track them together.
  • Ask customers: add "How did you find us?" to your enquiry form and include "ChatGPT / AI assistant" as an option.
  • Monthly prompt check: keep a list of 10 questions customers would ask and record whether each AI tool mentions you.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Publishing hundreds of thin AI-written pages — they rarely get indexed or quoted and can hurt the whole site.
  • Different addresses or phone numbers on different platforms.
  • Hiding prices and key facts inside images or PDFs only.
  • Blocking all bots at the CDN level without realising it blocks search crawlers too.
  • Ignoring reviews because "our work speaks for itself" — AI can only read what is written down.

30-day AI visibility checklist

  • Ask 3 AI tools about your business and record the answers
  • Make name, address, phone and hours identical everywhere
  • Complete every field in Google Business Profile, add 10+ photos
  • Collect 10–20 genuine Google reviews and reply to all
  • List the business on 4–5 relevant directories
  • Add LocalBusiness / Organization and FAQ schema
  • Rewrite top service pages with an answer-first opening
  • Check robots.txt and CDN bot settings
  • Submit sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
  • Set up an "AI Assistants" channel in GA4 and re-check AI answers after 30 days

Frequently asked questions

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO, sometimes called AI search optimisation or LLM SEO, is the work of making your business and content easy for AI tools like Google AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity to find, trust and quote. It builds on normal SEO: crawlable pages, clear content, structured data, reviews and consistent business information.

Do I need special optimisation for Google AI Overviews?

Google says no special markup or files are required. Pages need to be indexed and eligible to show a snippet in normal Search. Following SEO fundamentals, writing helpful content and keeping your business information accurate is the best preparation.

Why does ChatGPT not mention my business?

Common reasons are that your site is new or not indexed in the search sources it uses, your business has few reviews or third-party mentions, your details are inconsistent across the web, or your pages do not clearly state what you do and where. Submitting your sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools and allowing OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt also helps.

Does llms.txt improve AI rankings?

There is no strong evidence that it does. Google has said it does not use llms.txt for Search. It is harmless and quick to add, but reviews, accurate business information and clear content have far more impact.

How long does it take to appear in AI answers?

After fixing your profile, reviews and content, changes often take a few weeks to a few months to show, because search indexes and AI tools refresh at different speeds. Local businesses with complete profiles and steady new reviews usually see the fastest improvement.
