AI Chatbot Development Cost in India (2026): What You Actually Pay For

Chatbot quotes in India range from cheap template bots to large custom projects. Here is what each part of the cost covers, which parts are one-time and which are monthly, and what drives the price up or down.

How much does an AI chatbot cost in India?

The cost of an AI chatbot has two parts: a one-time development cost for setup, training on your data, design and integrations, and ongoing running costs for AI model usage, WhatsApp messaging if used, hosting and maintenance. At CodeServe, an AI website chatbot trained on your website and documents starts from ₹15,000 one-time; AI WhatsApp assistants start from ₹24,999 one-time setup, custom integrations are quoted based on scope, and usage costs are billed at actual rates.

The two parts of chatbot cost

Many businesses compare chatbot quotes only on the setup price and are surprised later by monthly bills. Always separate:

One-time development cost

Development covers the work to make the chatbot useful for your business:

  • Collecting and cleaning your information — website pages, PDFs, price lists, FAQs.
  • Setting up retrieval so the bot answers from your data (see RAG explained).
  • Writing the bot's instructions: tone, what it must not answer, when to hand over.
  • Lead capture fields and where leads are sent (email, Google Sheets, CRM, WhatsApp).
  • Branding the chat widget and installing it on your site.
  • Testing with real customer questions and fixing weak answers.

At CodeServe, an AI website chatbot starts from ₹15,000 one-time, including training on your website and documents, lead capture, branding and 30 days of support. AI WhatsApp assistants start from ₹24,999 one-time setup, and custom assistants are quoted after understanding your channels and integrations.

Ongoing running costs

AI model usage

AI providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google charge based on how much text is processed — roughly, the length of questions, retrieved information and answers. A business with a few hundred conversations a month pays far less than one with tens of thousands. Model choice matters too: smaller, faster models cost much less than the most advanced ones and are often enough for customer FAQs. Prices change frequently, so check the provider's current pricing page.

WhatsApp messaging

For WhatsApp chatbots, Meta charges for business messages based on message category (such as marketing, utility or authentication) and the customer's country, with some service conversations free under current rules. Rules and rates change, so check Meta's current WhatsApp Business pricing or ask your provider. More detail is in our WhatsApp Business API guide.

Hosting and maintenance

Simple chatbots may run on low-cost hosting; chatbots with many documents or integrations may need a database and more server resources. Maintenance — keeping answers current and reviewing conversations — is what keeps quality high after launch.

What makes a chatbot cost more

  • Number of channels: website only vs website + WhatsApp + Instagram.
  • Integrations: booking systems, CRMs, e-commerce stores, your own software.
  • Actions, not just answers: checking order status, creating bookings or updating records.
  • Volume and size of documents the bot must search.
  • Languages: supporting and testing multiple Indian languages.
  • Security and access control: internal assistants with staff roles and sensitive data.
  • Custom dashboards and reports for conversations and leads.

DIY tools vs custom development

Ready-made chatbot builders can be set up quickly for a monthly subscription and suit simple FAQ use. Custom development costs more upfront but gives you control over the AI model, data, integrations and branding, and avoids per-seat or per-feature subscription increases as you grow. A practical approach: start simple, measure how many conversations and leads the bot handles, then invest more where it clearly pays back.

How to budget and avoid surprises

  1. Estimate monthly conversations from current WhatsApp, call and website enquiry volume.
  2. Ask for a written estimate of monthly AI and messaging costs at that volume.
  3. Set a usage limit or alert with the AI provider.
  4. Make sure accounts with the AI provider and Meta are in your business name.
  5. Plan a few hours a month for reviewing conversations and updating answers.

Questions to ask before accepting a quote

  • Which AI model will be used, and can it be changed later?
  • Does the bot answer only from our data? What happens when it doesn't know?
  • Which costs are one-time and which are monthly?
  • Who owns the code, data and accounts?
  • How do we update answers ourselves?
  • What support is included after launch?

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to get an AI chatbot?

A ready-made chatbot builder with a monthly plan, or a simple custom FAQ chatbot trained on your website. At CodeServe, AI website chatbots start from ₹15,000 one-time plus usage-based AI costs.

Are there monthly costs after development?

Usually yes: AI model usage, WhatsApp message charges for WhatsApp bots, hosting where needed, and optional maintenance. Ask for an estimate based on your expected number of conversations.

Is a WhatsApp chatbot more expensive than a website chatbot?

Generally yes, because it needs the official WhatsApp Business API setup and Meta charges for certain business messages, in addition to AI usage costs.

How can I reduce AI chatbot running costs?

Use a smaller AI model for routine questions, keep answers concise, retrieve only the relevant parts of your documents, and route complex conversations to staff.

Who should own the chatbot accounts?

Your business should own the AI provider account, the WhatsApp Business account and the data. The developer can be given access, but ownership should stay with you.
