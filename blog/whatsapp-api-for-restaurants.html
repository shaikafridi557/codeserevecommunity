WhatsApp Business API for Restaurants: Direct Orders, Bookings and Repeat Customers

Aggregator apps bring new customers, but they also take a big cut of every order. The WhatsApp Business API helps restaurants take repeat orders and table bookings directly — automatically, even at peak hours.

Most restaurants already get orders and reservation requests on WhatsApp — typed by hand, at the busiest time of the day. The WhatsApp Business API turns that chat into an ordering and booking system that runs itself, and helps you keep regular customers ordering directly instead of through aggregators.

Why restaurants use WhatsApp for orders

  • Lower commission. Food delivery apps commonly charge restaurants a commission of roughly 15–30% per order. A repeat customer who orders on WhatsApp costs you a few paise in message charges.
  • You own the customer. You get the customer's number and order history, so you can bring them back.
  • No app to download. Every customer already has WhatsApp.
  • Fewer calls at peak hours. The bot takes the order while staff cook and serve.
Best of both: keep aggregators for discovery and new customers, and use WhatsApp for the regulars who already know you. Put a "Order direct on WhatsApp" card in every delivery bag.

Checklist: what a good restaurant WhatsApp setup includes

  • Menu with categories, photos, prices and "sold out" marking
  • Delivery area and minimum order checks
  • UPI or payment-gateway link, plus COD confirmation
  • Kitchen screen, printer or staff group alert for every order
  • Automatic order status messages
  • Table booking with reminders
  • Feedback request and Google review link
  • Simple daily report: orders, revenue, repeat customers

How it looks in practice: a cloud kitchen

A customer who ordered last week through an aggregator finds a card in the bag: "Order direct on WhatsApp and get 10% off." They message "Hi". The bot greets them by name next time, shows the menu, takes the order and a UPI payment, and sends the ticket to the kitchen. Ten minutes later: "Your order is out for delivery 🛵." On Sunday the customer gets one weekend-special message — and orders again.

What it costs a restaurant

Meta charges per message. In India, order-status updates are utility messages (about ₹0.115 each) and offers are marketing messages (about ₹0.86 each), plus GST. Chat replies are free for the first 1,000 a month per number. A restaurant handling 1,000 WhatsApp orders a month typically spends a few hundred to a few thousand rupees on Meta charges — mostly on offers. Details: WhatsApp API pricing in India.

Compare that with commission: on 1,000 orders averaging ₹400, a 20% commission is ₹80,000 a month.

How to set it up

  1. Verify your business on Meta Business (GST or FSSAI/trade licence and a website that shows the same name and address help).
  2. Connect a number — ideally the one printed on your menu and Google Business Profile.
  3. Set up your menu as a catalogue or menu flow with categories, prices and photos.
  4. Connect payments and the kitchen — UPI/payment gateway, kitchen screen or printer, delivery staff group.
  5. Create templates for order status, reservation reminders and offers, and get them approved.
  6. Promote it: QR code on tables and bags, WhatsApp button on your website and Google profile, Instagram bio link.

How to get customers ordering on WhatsApp

  • Table QR codes that open your WhatsApp chat with the message "Menu" pre-filled.
  • Bag inserts and stickers on every aggregator order: "Order direct on WhatsApp — 10% off your next order."
  • Google Business Profile: add the WhatsApp link and post your weekly specials.
  • Instagram bio and stories with a direct WhatsApp link.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads on Instagram and Facebook — conversations started from these ads open a free messaging window.
  • Staff script: "Next time, just message us on WhatsApp — it's faster."

What to measure

  • Direct WhatsApp orders per week and their share of total orders
  • Repeat-order rate of WhatsApp customers
  • Average order value on WhatsApp vs aggregators
  • Commission saved each month
  • Offer opt-outs and blocks — if these rise, send fewer offers

Mistakes restaurants make

  • Sending daily offers — customers block you and your quality rating drops. One or two a week is plenty.
  • No human fallback for complaints or special requests.
  • Menu prices in the bot not updated when the real menu changes.
  • Using unofficial bulk-messaging tools that get the number banned.

Frequently asked questions

Can restaurants take orders on WhatsApp automatically?

Yes. With the WhatsApp Business API a bot can show the menu, take the order and address, collect a UPI payment and send the ticket to the kitchen, with automatic status updates to the customer.

How much does WhatsApp ordering cost a restaurant?

Meta charges per message: about ₹0.115 for order-status updates and about ₹0.86 for offers in India, plus GST, with 1,000 free chat replies a month per number. Setup is usually a one-time cost.

Should restaurants stop using Swiggy and Zomato?

Not usually. Aggregators are good for reaching new customers. WhatsApp works best for repeat customers who already know you, so you avoid commission on those orders.

Can customers book a table on WhatsApp?

Yes. The bot collects the date, time and number of guests, confirms the booking and sends a reminder before the visit.
