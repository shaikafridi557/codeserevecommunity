Why hire through CodeServe instead of a single freelancer?

Hiring an individual freelancer can be cheap, but it is risky if they get busy, fall sick or disappear mid-project. Working with our team reduces that risk.

Matched to your project

We pick the developer whose skills fit your work, instead of one person trying to do everything.

Backup and continuity

If your developer is unavailable, another team member who knows the project can continue the work.

Review and testing

Work is reviewed and tested before it reaches you, so fewer bugs and less back-and-forth.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to hire a freelance developer in Hyderabad?

It depends on the skill, experience and engagement model. We give a clear quote for a fixed project, an hourly rate or a monthly dedicated developer after understanding your requirement.

Can I hire a developer for just a few hours?

Yes. Our hourly model is ideal for small fixes, updates and urgent issues. You pay only for the time used and receive time reports.

Do you work with clients outside Hyderabad and India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across India and abroad, and communicate over WhatsApp, email, Google Meet or Slack.

Can agencies hire you for white label work?

Yes. We build websites and apps under your agency's brand and never contact your clients directly. See our white label development service.

Who owns the code?

You do. After payment, all code, designs and files created for you belong to you.

How do you keep my project confidential?

We only use your information for your project and can sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share details.
