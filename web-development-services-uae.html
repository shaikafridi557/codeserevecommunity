Web Development & WhatsApp Automation for UAE Businesses

Team based in India · Only 1h30 time difference · Fixed quotes in AED

Almost the same working day

The UAE is 1h30 behind India, and neither country changes its clocks. That makes India one of the easiest places for Gulf businesses to work with remotely.

A 10 am meeting in Dubai is 11:30 am in Hyderabad. We can join calls during your full working day, answer WhatsApp messages quickly and turn around changes without waiting overnight.

We also understand the Gulf working week: UAE government and many private businesses work Monday to Friday with a shorter Friday, while some companies still keep different weekends. We agree your preferred working days and response times at the start of the project, so nobody waits on a message during their weekend.

WhatsApp is how your customers talk

In the UAE, customers often prefer to message a business on WhatsApp rather than fill in a form or send an email. Automation makes sure every message gets a fast, helpful reply.

Instant replies

Greetings, FAQs, prices, location and opening hours answered automatically in English or Arabic.

Real estate leads

Capture property enquiries, ask qualifying questions and send them straight to the right agent.

Bookings & reminders

Confirmations and reminders for clinics, salons, restaurants and hospitality.

Order updates

Order confirmations and delivery updates from your WooCommerce or Shopify store.

We use the official WhatsApp Business Platform. See our WhatsApp automation service and our guide to the WhatsApp Business API in the UAE.

Arabic & English bilingual websites

A UAE website often needs to serve both Arabic and English speakers. We build bilingual sites with a true right-to-left Arabic layout — not just translated text — including mirrored navigation, correctly aligned forms and Arabic-friendly fonts.

Each language gets its own page titles, descriptions and URLs so both versions can be found on Google. You provide or approve the Arabic copy; we make sure it displays correctly on every device.

  • Right-to-left (RTL) Arabic layout
  • Language switcher on every page
  • Separate SEO for Arabic and English
  • WhatsApp and click-to-call buttons
  • Fast loading on mobile networks
  • Google Maps and location pages

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between the UAE and India?

The UAE is only 1 hour 30 minutes behind India, and neither country uses daylight saving time. Our working days almost completely overlap, so calls and quick changes are easy to schedule.

Can you build Arabic and English websites?

Yes. We build bilingual websites with a proper right-to-left (RTL) Arabic layout, a language switcher and separate SEO titles and descriptions for each language. You provide or approve the Arabic translation.

Do you set up the WhatsApp Business API for UAE companies?

Yes. We set up the official WhatsApp Business Platform, connect it to your website, CRM or store, and build automated replies, booking confirmations, lead capture and broadcasts to opted-in customers. Meta bills message charges directly to your account.

Do you quote in AED?

Yes. We send fixed quotes in AED for the agreed scope and accept Wise, PayPal, Payoneer or bank transfer, usually with a 50% advance and the balance on delivery.

Which UAE industries do you work with?

Our services suit real estate brokers and developers, hospitality and restaurants, clinics, e-commerce brands, training institutes and service businesses — anywhere customers expect fast replies and an easy mobile experience.

Do you have an office in Dubai?

No. CodeServe is based in Hyderabad, India, and works with UAE and Gulf clients remotely. The small time difference makes this very practical.
