Team based in India · 4h30–5h30 ahead of UK time · Fixed quotes in GBP

A practical time-zone fit

India is 4h30 ahead of the UK in summer (BST) and 5h30 ahead in winter (GMT). That makes day-to-day collaboration straightforward.

A 9:30 am UK stand-up is mid-afternoon in Hyderabad, so we can discuss changes in your morning and often have them ready to review the same day. Urgent questions get answered during your working hours, and we send a written summary at the end of each day.

Built with UK GDPR in mind

Privacy is a common concern for UK businesses outsourcing development. These are standard on our UK projects:

  • Cookie consent banners that block non-essential cookies until the visitor agrees
  • Privacy policy and cookie policy pages (you provide or approve the wording)
  • Contact and checkout forms that collect only what you need
  • SSL on every site and secure handling of admin access
  • Clear documentation of which third-party tools receive data
  • Access removed at the end of a project if you ask

We are not legal advisers — please confirm your specific GDPR obligations with your own solicitor or data protection adviser. We'll implement what they recommend.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between the UK and India?

India is 4 hours 30 minutes ahead of the UK during British Summer Time and 5 hours 30 minutes ahead during GMT. That gives a comfortable overlap: a UK morning meeting falls in the Indian afternoon.

Do you quote in pounds sterling?

Yes. We send fixed quotes in GBP for the agreed scope and accept Wise, PayPal, Payoneer or bank transfer. Projects normally start with a 50% advance, with the rest on delivery or by milestones.

Can you build UK GDPR-friendly websites?

We build with privacy in mind: cookie consent banners that block non-essential cookies until consent, privacy policy and cookie policy pages, secure forms, minimal data collection and clear data flows. GDPR compliance is ultimately a legal matter, so please confirm your obligations with your own adviser.

Do you work white label for UK agencies?

Yes. Many UK agencies need extra development capacity. We build websites, WooCommerce stores and automations under your brand, sign an NDA and stay invisible to your clients unless you want us on calls.

Is WhatsApp automation useful for UK businesses?

Yes, especially for service businesses, clinics, salons, trades and e-commerce stores whose customers already message them on WhatsApp. We set up the official WhatsApp Business Platform for booking confirmations, reminders and order updates.

Do you have an office in the UK?

No. Our team is based in Hyderabad, India, and works with UK clients remotely through video calls, email, Slack and WhatsApp.
