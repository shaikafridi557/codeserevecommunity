Why outsource web development to India?

India has a large pool of experienced developers and a long history of remote software work. Outsourcing can save money and time — but only if you choose the right partner. Here is an honest view.

The advantages

Lower development costs than most Western markets, strong skills in modern stacks (React, Node.js, Python, WordPress, Flutter), English as a working language and the ability to scale a team up or down.

The risks

Unclear scopes, poor communication, missed deadlines and code you cannot access. These usually come from choosing on price alone, without written agreements or regular updates.

How we reduce the risk

A written scope and fixed quote, a shared timeline, a private staging site you can check at any time, agreed update times, an NDA if you need one, and full hand-over of code and logins.

Engagement models

Choose the model that fits your project and budget. Every engagement starts with a free consultation.

Fixed-price project

Best for a clear scope such as a website, store or automation. You get a fixed quote in your currency, milestones and a delivery date.

Dedicated developer (monthly)

A developer who works on your product every month, reporting to you directly. Good for ongoing development, agencies and startups.

Hourly / on-demand

For maintenance, small changes and support. Work is logged and shared with you, so you only pay for the time used.

How we work with remote clients

Good communication matters more than location. This is how every remote project runs:

Discovery call

A Google Meet or Zoom call to understand your goals, followed by a written scope and fixed quote.

Plan & design

Wireframes or designs for approval, with a shared timeline and milestones.

Build on staging

You review progress on a private staging link and get regular updates on WhatsApp, Slack or email.

Launch & hand-over

We go live, hand over code and logins after final payment, and support you after launch.

  • Agreed daily overlap hours for calls and quick replies
  • Updates on WhatsApp, Slack or email — whichever you prefer
  • Google Meet or Zoom calls for demos and decisions
  • Shared project tracking so you can see what is in progress
  • Mutual NDA before you share confidential details
  • You own the code, design files and accounts after full payment

Frequently asked questions

Where is CodeServe based?

Our team is based in Hyderabad, India. We work with clients in other countries remotely, using video calls, WhatsApp, Slack and email. We do not have offices outside India.

How do you handle time-zone differences?

We agree a daily overlap window at the start of the project and keep to it. India works well with the UAE and UK during the day, with Australia in our morning and with the USA in our evening or early morning.

Who owns the code and design?

You do. After full payment we hand over the source code, design files, logins and documentation. Domains, hosting and business accounts are registered in your name.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share confidential details, and we keep client work private unless you allow us to show it in our portfolio.

How do international payments work?

We send fixed quotes in your currency (USD, GBP, EUR, AED or AUD) and accept Wise, PayPal, Payoneer or bank transfer. Projects usually start with a 50% advance, with the rest on delivery or split into milestones for larger work.

Can you work as a white label partner for my agency?

Yes. We build websites, stores, apps and automations under your agency's brand, with no CodeServe branding and no direct contact with your client unless you ask for it.
