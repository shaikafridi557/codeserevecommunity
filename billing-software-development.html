Custom billing software vs ready-made apps

Ready-made billing apps are a good start for many small shops. Custom software makes sense when your process doesn't fit them. Here is an honest comparison.

Ready-made billing apps

  • Low monthly fee, quick to start
  • Standard features for common businesses
  • You adjust your process to the software
  • Limited changes to reports and workflows
  • Recurring subscription for every user or branch

Custom billing software

  • Built around your exact workflow
  • Only the features you need, so it stays simple
  • Connects to your website, WhatsApp or other tools
  • Reports designed for how you make decisions
  • Higher one-time cost, no per-user licence from us
A note on GST compliance: we build invoices and reports to support GST requirements, but rules such as e-invoicing and e-way bills depend on your business and turnover and can change. Please confirm your compliance requirements with your CA, and we'll build the software to match.

Frequently asked questions

Will the billing software be GST compliant?

We build GST-ready invoices, tax breakups and GST summary reports. Specific requirements like e-invoicing or e-way bills depend on your business and turnover, so we confirm them with you and your CA and build accordingly.

Can I use it without internet?

We can build an offline-capable version for counters that syncs to the cloud when the internet is back. Let us know during planning if your shop has unreliable internet.

Can you move my data from my current software or Excel?

Yes. We import products, customers, suppliers and opening balances from Excel or exports of your current software.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

The software is a one-time development cost. You pay for hosting (cloud server) yourself, usually a small monthly amount, and can optionally take a monthly support plan.

Does it work with barcode scanners and thermal printers?

Yes. We support common USB barcode scanners and thermal receipt printers, and A4 invoice printing.

How long does it take to build?

A focused billing and invoicing app usually takes 4–6 weeks. Inventory, POS and multi-branch features add time, which we confirm in the quote.
