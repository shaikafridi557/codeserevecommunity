Frequently asked questions

How much does website development cost in Hyderabad?

At CodeServe, a starter business website costs ₹9,999, a business website with CMS ₹19,999 and an e-commerce store ₹34,999. Custom web applications start from ₹49,999. Domain and hosting are extra and registered in your name.

How long does it take to build a business website?

A starter website takes about 7 days, a business website 10 to 14 days and an online store 15 to 21 days after we receive your logo, text and photos.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. Every website includes 15 days of free bug-fix support. After that, monthly care plans start at ₹1,999/month for updates, backups, security and small changes.

Can you add WhatsApp automation to my existing website?

Yes. We can connect the official WhatsApp Business API to your existing website or online store for enquiries, order updates, booking reminders and automated replies, without rebuilding the site.

Do you work with clients outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Our team is based in KPHB, Kukatpally, and we work with businesses across India and with clients in the USA, UK, UAE and Australia through video calls, WhatsApp and email.
