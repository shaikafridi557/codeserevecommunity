Time zones: how we overlap with US hours

IST is 9h30 ahead of US Eastern time in summer (EDT) and 10h30 ahead in winter (EST) — and 12h30 to 13h30 ahead of Pacific time.

In practice, our evening (around 7–10 pm IST) lines up with US Eastern mornings, and our early morning can meet US Pacific late afternoons. We agree a fixed overlap window — usually one to two hours a day — for calls and quick questions, and send a written update at the end of each Indian working day so you can review it when you start yours.

Daylight saving changes in the US (India does not change its clocks) shift these times by an hour twice a year; we remind you and adjust the meeting schedule.

A note on ADA and accessibility

Many US businesses are asked about website accessibility and the Americans with Disabilities Act. We follow WCAG 2.1 AA practices when we design and build, and we can review an existing website and fix common issues such as missing alt text, low contrast and forms that can't be used with a keyboard.

We are developers, not lawyers: whether your site meets a specific legal standard is something to confirm with your own legal adviser. What we can promise is that accessibility is part of how we build, not an afterthought.

Frequently asked questions

What working hours can you overlap with in the US?

India Standard Time is 9 hours 30 minutes ahead of US Eastern time during daylight saving and 10 hours 30 minutes ahead in winter (12h30–13h30 ahead of Pacific). We schedule calls in our evening or early morning so they fall in your morning or late afternoon, and agree a fixed overlap window at the start.

Do you quote in US dollars?

Yes. After a discovery call we send a fixed quote in USD for the agreed scope. Payment is usually 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, or milestone-based for larger projects, via Wise, PayPal, Payoneer or bank transfer.

Can you help with ADA and WCAG accessibility?

We build with WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines in mind — proper headings, alt text, colour contrast, keyboard navigation and form labels — and can audit an existing site. Accessibility and ADA obligations are legal questions, so please confirm your requirements with your own legal adviser.

Do you work on Shopify and WooCommerce stores for US brands?

Yes. We set up and customise Shopify and WooCommerce stores, including themes, apps or plugins, payment gateways such as Stripe and PayPal, shipping rules, sales tax settings and order automation.

Is there an office in the United States?

No. CodeServe is based in Hyderabad, India, and works with US clients fully remotely through video calls, Slack, email and WhatsApp.
