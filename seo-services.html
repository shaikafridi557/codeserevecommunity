Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results?

Local SEO improvements on Google Maps can show in a few weeks. Ranking for competitive keywords usually takes 3 to 6 months of consistent work, depending on your competition, website age and reviews.

How much do SEO services cost in Hyderabad?

At CodeServe, monthly SEO is included in the Growth plan at ₹4,999/month and the Pro plan at ₹9,999/month, which adds ads management. One-time SEO audits are quoted based on website size.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No honest SEO company can guarantee rankings, because Google decides them. We commit to doing the work that improves rankings, and we report progress transparently every month.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and Google AI answers?

Yes. We make your business information consistent across the web, add structured data, improve reviews and citations, and write answer-first content, which are the signals AI search tools use to choose businesses.

Do you do SEO for businesses outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We work with businesses across India and internationally, but we specialise in local SEO for Hyderabad areas such as Kukatpally, KPHB, Miyapur, Madhapur and Hitech City.
